The ranch has a website now. The chickens have not commented, which I am taking as a good sign.

This is 44 Bar Ranch, a small family place in rural Texas. I raise chickens and turkeys, keep cattle, and live with dogs who have promoted themselves to management. The hens are the ones on a schedule. Eggs most mornings, if everybody cooperates and nobody left a gate open.

I will write here when something on the place is worth telling. A full basket. A turkey with a complaint. A cow on the wrong side of the fence. Nothing fancy.

Glad you stopped by.

— Jay
